    Etching models into silicon is the logical endpoint of the "specialize everything" trend. We've gone from general-purpose CPUs to GPUs to TPUs to ASICs, and now effectively hard-wiring weights. The interesting question is whether the model architecture freeze required for tape-out is acceptable given how fast the field moves.

    My guess: this works brilliantly for inference at scale where you know exactly which model you'll run for the next 3–5 years. Training and research labs won't touch it.
